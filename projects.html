RxEase: Drag-and-Drop Prescription Automation

My pharmacist Jon once mentioned how there should be a quicker way to handle new prescriptions—something that would help us fill meds faster without all the manual typing. That’s what inspired me to build RxEase. It’s a simple tool where you can just drag and drop a prescription document, and it automatically picks out important info like SIGs (instructions), medication names, and NPI numbers. Everything gets sorted into a clean digital format, so it saves time and makes things a bit smoother behind the counter.

MediMinder: Helping you stay on track of your medication

As a pharmacy technician, I’ve had countless elderly patients tell me they’re unsure about what medication they’re picking up, what it’s for, or when it needs to be refilled. A lot of the apps available today aren’t built with older users in mind—they’re cluttered, confusing, and hard to navigate. So I decided to create a solution focused on intuitive user interaction and simplified UI. The idea is simple: by scanning their Rx label, users get a clear view of what their medication does and when it should be refilled—no complicated menus, no tiny text, just a smooth, straightforward experience built for ease and confidence.
